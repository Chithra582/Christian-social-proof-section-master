# Testimonial Card Styling Skill

## Overview
Styles individual review cards with polished aesthetics, managing quote text, verified buyer badges, and star rating visuals.

## Operational Workflow
1. Bind theme color palette to card background and text elements.
2. Validate stylesheet rules via `css-rule-validator`.
3. Ensure visual alignment of author avatars and rating badges.
